Transparent AI use, on your terms

Pair with your Thesisfy account: in Thesisfy open Settings & Privacy → Browser extension and generate a code.

Checking…

While a writing session is active in Thesisfy, this extension reports on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Le Chat:

  • that you visited the site, and for how long
  • a fingerprint of text you copy (never the text)
  • that you sent a prompt

Nothing is reported outside an active session. Change your choices in Thesisfy → Settings & Privacy.
